# Insurance-Risk-and-Claim-Analysis
1. Project Overview

This project analyzes insurance risk and claim patterns using policyholder and claims data. The goal is to identify trends in claim amount, customer demographics, vehicle usage, and education level to support better risk assessment and business decisions.

The dashboard was built to help insurance companies:

Understand claim behavior

Identify high-risk customer segments

Analyze claim amount distribution

Improve premium pricing strategies

2. Tools & Technologies Used

Power BI – Dashboard development & data visualization

Excel / CSV – Data source

DAX – Measures and calculated fields

Data Cleaning & Transformation – Power Query

4. Dashboard Insights
1️⃣ Claim Analysis by Car Use

Compared claim amounts between Private and Commercial vehicles

Private vehicles show higher overall claim distribution

2️⃣ Claim Count by Car Make

Ford and Chevrolet have the highest claim counts

Helps identify vehicle models associated with higher risk

3️⃣ Policies by Age Group

Highest policy holders: 26–35 and 36–45 age groups

Younger age groups show moderate claim frequency

4️⃣ Policies by Car Year

Trend analysis shows increasing policy numbers over recent car models

Newer vehicles show significant policy growth

5️⃣ Policies by Education & Marital Status

Married customers have higher total claim amounts

Bachelor’s degree holders show highest claim contribution

6️⃣ Kids Driving Impact

Customers with more kids show different claim patterns

Helps in risk segmentation
